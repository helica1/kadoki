# Kadoki Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 23, 2026_

Kadoki is a reader for Japanese audiobooks and e-books. It is made by one developer, has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and the developer runs no servers. **The developer does not collect any data about you.**

## What stays on your device

Your books, audiobooks, subtitles, dictionaries, reading positions, statistics, flashcards and settings are stored only on your devices. Transcription (turning an audiobook into text), dictionary lookups and Apple Translation run on the device.

## Optional features that contact other services

Some features send data to a third party, and only when you turn them on. The developer never receives this data.

- **AI features (summaries, proofreading, explanations, translations):** if you enter your own API key for Anthropic or OpenRouter, Kadoki sends the needed passages of the book you are reading, and your questions, to that provider using your key. Their privacy policies apply ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy), [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/privacy)).
- **AI pictures:** if you enter your own fal.ai or OpenAI key, Kadoki sends picture descriptions written from the book to that provider ([fal.ai](https://fal.ai/privacy), [OpenAI](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy)).
- **Place names and maps:** when you open a real-world place mentioned in a book, Kadoki looks up that place name on OpenStreetMap (Nominatim) and Japanese Wikipedia, and loads map images from OpenStreetMap.
- **Google Drive sync:** if you connect Google Drive, Kadoki copies your library and progress to your own Google Drive account, where only you can see it ([Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy)).
- **Anki:** cards you send go to AnkiMobile or AnkiDroid on your device, or to desktop Anki on your local network.

You can remove an API key or disconnect Google Drive at any time in Preferences.

## Apple Watch

The Watch app receives titles, subtitles and positions from your iPhone directly. Nothing passes through the developer.

## Children

Kadoki does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
